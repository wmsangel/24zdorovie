Aesthetic and injectable cosmetology promises a lot — from "ten years younger" to "a lift without surgery." Reality varies: **injections (botulinum toxin, fillers) and lasers have good evidence; device lifting and cryolipolysis are moderate; care procedures like HydraFacial are cosmetic and short-lived.** Here is a method-by-method look, without inflated expectations.

## Injections: the best evidence

**Botulinum toxin** relaxes expression muscles and smooths movement lines (forehead, glabella, crow's feet). Studied for decades, predictable, lasting 3–4 months. Nearly all risks (asymmetry, eyelid ptosis) relate to technique, not the product — hence an experienced practitioner matters.

**Fillers** (usually hyaluronic acid) restore volume and soften deep folds. The effect is immediate and lasts from months to a year or two. The key risks are vascular complications from incorrect injection — again a matter of skill.

## Device lifting and contouring

**SMAS lifting (ultrasound) and RF lifting** stimulate collagen and give moderate tightening; the effect develops over 2–3 months and lasts a limited time. Know the limit: with marked tissue descent this is only slight improvement, not an alternative to surgery.

**Cryolipolysis** (freezing fat cells) has moderate evidence for localized deposits — reducing a pinch by a few millimetres. It is contouring, not weight loss.

<Callout type="warning" title="Contraindications are serious">
  Device and injectable methods have contraindications: pregnancy, certain conditions, some medications, active inflammation in the area. A competent practitioner takes a history and states a realistic result honestly. Promises of a "permanent effect in one session" are a red flag.
</Callout>

## What to choose

Start with the goal — movement wrinkles, pigmentation, volume or contour: each has its own method, and "do everything at once" is rarely needed. Then it is not the device brand that decides but a licensed clinic and a practitioner experienced in that procedure. Home gadgets and skincare do not replace this, but procedures do not cancel the basics either — sunscreen and retinoids.

## Bottom line

Cosmetology works, but selectively and temporarily: injections and lasers predictably, device lifting and cryolipolysis moderately, care procedures cosmetically. What most affects the result is not the choice of a trendy device but the practitioner's hands and sober expectations.
