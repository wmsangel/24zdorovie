All of 24zdorovie is open and free: no subscription, no "pay to keep reading" walls. The project runs on enthusiasm and small voluntary contributions from readers. If an explainer, calculator or recipe was useful to you, here are two ways to say thanks. Both are entirely optional.

## Where the money goes

Contributions cover the project's direct costs: the domain and hosting, generating OG images and running the calculators, and access to the research databases and medical sources we cite. Anything beyond that goes into producing new material and updating older pieces. We don't charge for access to articles and we don't hide content behind donations.

<Callout type="warning" title="Important about transfers">
  - Donations are **voluntary** and unlock nothing: the whole site is already open.
  - On-chain transfers are **irreversible**. If you pick the wrong network or asset, the funds cannot be recovered — not by us, not by anyone.
  - Send **only** the listed asset on the listed network. Don't use addresses from third-party "wrappers" or aggregators (e.g. OKX X Layer) — ordinary wallets can't send to those.
  - The first time, send a **small test amount** and confirm it arrived.
  - Gifting cryptocurrency is a **taxable event** in some countries. That's the sender's responsibility.
</Callout>

Thanks for reading. Even just a link to us in your article or a forum reply helps the project as much as a donation — it brings new readers in from search.
