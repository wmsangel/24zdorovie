Our single editorial address is **hello@24zdorovie.com**. It is a working inbox, and a human reads it.

## What to write about

- **An error or inaccuracy in an article.** Send the link and a quote of the disputed passage, ideally with a link to the source that contradicts it. We will check it, and if the error is confirmed we will fix the text and update the revision date.
- **A topic suggestion or a question.** Tell us what is unclear or missing on the site. We collect these messages and use them when planning coverage.
- **Partnerships.** Expert review of our articles, joint projects, content collaborations — a couple of lines about you and your proposal is enough.
- **Advertising.** Placement requests, media kit and rates go to the same address; please put "Advertising" in the subject line. Formats and restrictions are described on our advertising page.

## Response times

We reply within 3 working days. Reports of errors in published articles are handled first.

## What we cannot help with

We do not provide medical consultations, interpret test results, diagnose conditions or recommend treatments and drug regimens. Those questions belong with a clinician — see our medical disclaimer for details.
