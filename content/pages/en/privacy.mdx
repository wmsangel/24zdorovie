## Who processes the data

The 24zdorovie.com website and the 24zdorovie editorial team. For any question about data, write to [info@24zdorovie.com](mailto:info@24zdorovie.com). We reply within 30 days, usually much sooner.

## What data we receive

**Data you give us.** Your email address if you subscribe to the newsletter, and the contents of your message if you write to us. That is all we ask for: there is no registration, no user account and no health questionnaire on this site.

**Technical data your browser sends.** Whenever a page opens, the server receives an IP address, browser type and version, operating system, language, the page requested and the referring page. This is how the web works — without it the browser could not fetch the page at all.

**Analytics data.** Anonymous statistics: which pages get read, how long people stay, what devices they use. It tells us which articles are useful and what is broken. We look at site-wide numbers, not at individual people.

<Callout type="info" title="What we do not do">
  We do not collect special-category health data — conditions, diagnoses, medications. Reading our article on vitamin D does not create a record saying "this user is deficient in vitamin D": there is simply no mechanism here that links pages to a reader's identity.
</Callout>

## Why we need it

- **To serve the site.** Technical data makes the page load and remembers your theme and language between visits.
- **To understand what gets read.** Statistics show which topics readers need and which articles should be rewritten or removed.
- **To send the newsletter.** Your address is used only to send new articles. We never sell the list or rent it out to advertisers.
- **To show advertising.** Advertising pays for the editorial work and keeps every article free to read.
- **To answer your email.** Correspondence is kept for as long as it takes to resolve the matter.

## Cookies and browser storage

**Technical.** The site stores your chosen colour theme in `localStorage` (key `24zdorovie-theme`). It is not a cookie, never leaves your device and is not shared with anyone.

**Analytics.** Set by statistics services to tell a returning visitor from a new one.

**Advertising.** Set by ad networks to serve ads and cap how often you see the same one.

Analytics and advertising cookies can be switched off in your browser settings or with an ad blocker. This does not affect reading — all content stays available.

## Affiliate links

Some product links are affiliate links: if you follow one and buy something, the shop pays us a commission and the price for you does not change. Such links are labelled, and following one tells the shop you arrived from our site. What the shop does with that is governed by its own policy. Details are on the [Advertise](/en/advertising) page.

## How long we keep data

Your email address: for as long as you are subscribed, plus a short period after unsubscribing so we do not accidentally add you again. Correspondence: while the matter is open and up to a year after. Anonymous statistics: per the analytics services' terms, typically 14 to 26 months. Server logs: a few weeks.

## Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, request correction or deletion, withdraw consent for the newsletter, or object to processing. Email [info@24zdorovie.com](mailto:info@24zdorovie.com) from the address in question. You can also unsubscribe using the link in any newsletter — no explanation needed.

## Children

This site is not intended for children under 16 and does not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us their data, email us and we will delete it.

## Security

The site runs over HTTPS. Only the editorial team can access subscriber addresses. Perfect security does not exist online, which is exactly why we deliberately collect as little as possible — what we never hold cannot leak.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this page, for example when we add a new service. The date of the last change is shown at the top. Significant changes are announced in the newsletter.
