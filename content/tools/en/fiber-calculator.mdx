    a: "You need both, and whole foods contain them together. Soluble fiber (oats, legumes, apples) forms a gel, slows sugar absorption and lowers LDL cholesterol. ## How the calculation works

The calculator computes the target two ways and takes the higher one, but never below the EFSA minimum of 25 g.

The first way is the **IOM reference values** by sex and age: 38 g for men under 50, 25 g for women; after 50, 30 and 21 g. The second is the **14 g per 1000 kcal rule** — the very rule IOM used to derive its figures, which is why a higher-calorie diet has a higher fiber target.

<Callout type="science" title="Why IOM and EFSA differ">
  IOM ties the target to calories and sex; EFSA sets a single 25 g minimum for all adults. Neither number is an "ideal" — both are thresholds below which things are reliably worse: EFSA's criterion is normal bowel function, IOM's higher figures track reduced cardiovascular risk. The spread between them is the honest width of the "target".
</Callout>

## The real problem is the gap, not the target

Arguing over 25 versus 38 grams is nearly pointless while the average person eats around 15–18. It is this gap — not the choice between bran and psyllium — that decides whether you get the benefit.

Legumes are the densest source: one serving of lentils covers almost a third of the daily gap. The skin of fruit and vegetables holds a meaningful share of the fiber, so there is no reason to peel them without cause.

## How to build up without bloating

Fiber is fermented by bacteria — gas on a sudden increase is normal and settles in 1–2 weeks. Two rules remove most of the discomfort: build up gradually and raise your water intake in proportion. Fiber works by holding fluid; without drinking, a sudden jump can actually worsen constipation.

## Who this does not apply to

In irritable bowel syndrome, active inflammatory bowel disease, strictures or the early period after gastrointestinal surgery, the target and type of fiber are set individually with a doctor. A blanket "more fiber" can worsen symptoms in these situations.
