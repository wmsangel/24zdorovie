## What it is

Magnesium is a mineral cofactor for hundreds of enzyme reactions — from producing energy to the work of muscles and nerves and building bone. The body holds about 25 grams, most of it in the skeleton. It is not a fashionable add-on but a basic element that a normal diet usually supplies.

The main trap is the marketing of magnesium "for everything" — from fatigue to stress to cramps. The one well-proven role of the supplement is to close a shortfall where one exists.

## Who falls short

In a healthy person on an ordinary diet, overt deficiency is uncommon. It is more frequent in risk groups: gut disease (celiac, Crohn's), type 2 diabetes, alcohol dependence, older age, and long-term use of proton-pump inhibitors or diuretics. Those are the people who benefit — on indication, not "for prevention".

## Forms and absorption

- **Citrate, glycinate, malate** — absorb well and are gentler on the gut.
- **Oxide** — cheap, but absorbs poorly and is more laxative (it is often used as a laxative for that reason).

In practice the difference between "advanced" forms is small for most people; the real question is whether you have a reason to take it at all.

## Food sources

Nuts and seeds, legumes, whole grains, leafy green vegetables, dark chocolate. A varied plate usually covers the daily requirement without any capsules.

## Cautions and common myths

<Callout type="warning" title="When to be careful">
  In kidney disease magnesium can accumulate — take it only with a doctor. Excess from supplements causes diarrhea and abdominal cramping. Separate the timing from bisphosphonates and some antibiotics.
</Callout>

- **"Magnesium relieves any cramp."** The 2020 Cochrane review: no clinically meaningful benefit for common or age-related cramps.
- **"Magnesium treats stress and insomnia."** The evidence is weak; do not rely on it as a sleeping aid.
- **"More is better."** Above 350 mg/day from supplements without a reason brings only the risk of diarrhea, with no extra benefit.

## Proven vs hyped

- **Proven:** closing a confirmed deficiency and a shortfall in risk groups.
- **Hyped:** cramps, "anti-stress", sleep, fat loss, and the superiority of expensive forms over ordinary ones.
